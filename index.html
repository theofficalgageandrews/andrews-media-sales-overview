Follow-up after a lead is captured

Cold email positives

The reply posts to a Slack channel immediately with a fit check, next steps and a draft response, targeting a reply in 15 minutes or less. We scrape the phone number, add the lead to GHL, and call right away with a double dial. The goal on that call is to pitch and close. If no call books: timed touches over about 30 days, then a monthly useful message for 12 months, then recycle after 90 days.

Cold call leads

A bot builds a 3-email drip from the call transcript, notes and scraped web data on the prospect. Email 1 is the offer. Email 2 is a personalized audit. Email 3 is the break-off.

The sales call

  • Open: frame the call and confirm who decides.
  • Discovery: background, current state (deal value and volume), problem, goal, gap, urgency, other options, recap, permission to pitch, then a 1-to-10 score.
  • Pitch: research, then full revamp and batch shoot, then ads in parallel. Each step has a client walkthrough and a tie-down question.
  • Close: two models (full build-out or leaner). "Need to think" gets a three-question close and a refundable deposit that holds the filming slot.
